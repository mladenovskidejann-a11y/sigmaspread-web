How The Engine Works

A recency-weighted statistical model, not a black box

Step 1

Form-Weighted Model

Each side is scored from its last ~12 matches, recent form weighted higher than season-long averages, with small-sample teams shrunk toward the league mean.

Step 2

Market Comparison

Bookmaker odds are de-vigged into fair probabilities and compared against the model's own probabilities across 1X2, Over/Under and BTTS markets.

Step 3

News Adjustment

Confirmed injuries and suspensions apply a small, bounded adjustment. If nothing clears the value threshold that day, no signal is sent — ever.
